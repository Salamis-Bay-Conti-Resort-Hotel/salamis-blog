The Most Beautiful Bays of Northern Cyprus

Cyprus bays are renowned across the Mediterranean for their untouched beauty and crystal-clear waters. These hidden paradises of the island offer more than just golden sandy shores; they open the door to a vibrant, rich underwater world. Exploring the Cyprus bays can transform your holiday into a unique experience where tranquility, curiosity, and pure pleasure converge. Some of the most beautiful bays in Cyprus and their popular beaches include: Karpaz Peninsula bays: Golden Beach, Ayios Philon Beach, Ronnas Bay Kyrenia (Girne) bays: Escape Beach, Denizkızı Beach, Alagadi Beach Tatlısu and Bafra bays: Bafra Public Beach, Tatlısu coastline Güzelyurt and Lefke bays: Yeşilırmak Beach, Gemikonağı Beach If you’d like, let’s take a closer look at these stunning Cypriot coves and the various activities you can enjoy in these coastal paradises.

Karpaz Peninsula Bays

Traveling to the northernmost tip of the island brings you to the Karpaz Peninsula, a region celebrated for its vibrant, untamed natural life. The area remains largely untouched to preserve its delicate habitats. While walking along the fine sands of Karpaz, you will hear nothing but the gentle sound of waves and birdsong. For those seeking to immerse themselves in nature, these are undoubtedly among the most beautiful bays in Cyprus. Golden Beach: Renowned for its shimmering golden sand, this expansive shoreline and its shallow waters provide perfect swimming areas for everyone. Ayios Philon Beach: Located adjacent to the 12th-century Ayios Philon Church and its surrounding ancient ruins, this spot offers a peerless experience. Ronnas Bay: As a protected area where construction is strictly prohibited, Ronnas Bay is a sanctuary for nature lovers and campers. It is also a vital spot to observe green sea turtles in their natural habitat. Tatlısu and Bafra Bays As you head north from the Famagusta region, you are greeted by the enchanting coasts of Tatlısu and Bafra. In recent years, these areas have become increasingly popular among Northern Cyprus bays. Bafra is renowned for its well-organized coastline. In addition to premium hotel beaches, the area features a large public beach, open to everyone. Thanks to its shallow, calm Mediterranean waters, the beaches here provide a safe and relaxing experience, making them a top choice for families with children. For nature enthusiasts, the untouched small inlets along the Tatlısu coast are considered among the most beautiful bays in Cyprus. These hidden gems offer a highly romantic atmosphere for couples looking to escape the crowds and immerse themselves in nature. Furthermore, the rugged beauty of these Cyprus bays makes them a perennial favorite among campers and adventure seekers Bays in Güzelyurt and Lefke Region

Beyond the popular tourist hubs, the western coasts of Güzelyurt and Lefke offer a tranquil escape. Famous for their lush citrus groves, these regions are home to some of the most beautiful bays in Cyprus, characterized by cooler waters and rich underwater life that fascinates diving enthusiasts. Yeşilırmak (Limnitis) Bay: Situated at the westernmost point of the island, this bay is a fairy-tale destination where vibrant strawberry fields meet the deep blue sea. Gemikonağı (Karavostasi) Coast: As a symbol of Lefke, this coastline offers unique photo opportunities with its rustic old pier ruins and historical texture, making it one of the most picturesque Cyprus bays. Escape Beach: Located on the road between Kyrenia and Güzelyurt, this spot is one of the liveliest Cyprus bays in the region. With its shallow golden sands and extensive water sports facilities, it is a favorite for those seeking an active day by the sea. Exploring these diverse Cyprus bays allows you to witness the island’s western charm, where nature and history remain perfectly preserved.

Activities at the Bays

Northern Cyprus bays are perfect for a peaceful beach experience. However, beyond their tranquil atmosphere, they also offer exciting activities. Each of these Cyprus bays offers a unique experience. Swimming and Snorkeling If you are interested in underwater life, Cyprus bays are perfect for you. The clear waters make the underwater world visible like glass, offering a colorful experience. You can explore this world with a snorkel and mask or join scuba diving tours for a more professional experience. Boat Tours and Sunset Trips A Mediterranean holiday is not complete without a boat tour or a sunset cruise. Departing from the shore, these boats take you to popular Cyprus bays that are difficult to reach by land. These coastal areas are also known for their long walking paths.

Accommodation and Transportation Guide

Many of the most beautiful bays in Cyprus are accessible by private car. Staying in hotels near the coastline and renting a private car makes exploring these areas much easier. Car Rental and Travel Tips Renting a private car is by far the most comfortable way to travel around the island. This allows you to explore all the bays freely without depending on public transportation schedules.
