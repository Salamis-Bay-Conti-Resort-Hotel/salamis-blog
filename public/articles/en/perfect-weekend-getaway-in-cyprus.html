The Perfect Weekend Getaway in Cyprus

Leaving work on Friday evening, catching a flight, and waking up the next morning beside the warm Mediterranean... Cyprus is the perfect island for exactly this kind of short escape. You don't need a long holiday. Sometimes, just two days are enough to recharge. The warm Mediterranean waters, the aroma of freshly grilled halloumi, and streets steeped in history leave a lasting impression, even over a single weekend. In this guide, we've planned your weekend step by step, from your arrival on Friday to your departure on Sunday. It's designed for anyone who wants to enjoy a Cyprus holiday that's short, yet packed with memorable experiences. Here's the simplest way to experience the island in just two days. Friday: Arrival and Your First Evening Starting your weekend break in Cyprus on a Friday evening allows you to make the most of your time on the island. After checking into your hotel, rather than settling in for the evening, head out to explore your surroundings and start soaking up the island's unique atmosphere. For guests staying at Northern Cyprus hotels, Kyrenia and Nicosia are among the best places to spend your first evening. Kyrenia Harbour offers a truly captivating setting at sunset, with its historic stone buildings, elegant yachts and beautiful sea views. Enjoy fresh seafood, traditional Cypriot meze and local specialities at one of the harbour-front restaurants before taking a leisurely stroll along the waterfront. If you wish, stop to admire the illuminated exterior of Kyrenia Castleand capture a few memorable photos. If you're staying in Nicosia, take a short evening walk around the historic Arasta Bazaar. Sitting in the courtyard of Büyük Han with a coffee, listening to live music and taking in the historic surroundings is a wonderful way to begin your weekend. If your Northern Cyprus hotel offers a casino, spa or live entertainment, you may prefer to spend your first evening at the hotel, unwinding after your journey and recharging for the day ahead. Saturday Morning: Breakfast and the Sea

Mornings in Cyprus are best enjoyed at a slower pace. However, as the sea is at its calmest in the early hours, it's worth starting your day with breakfast before heading to the beach. A generous Cypriot breakfast is the perfect way to begin the day. Warm halloumi, olives, freshly baked bread and honey create a simple yet satisfying spread. Many hotels serve breakfast on terraces overlooking the sea, and among Cyprus hotels, those known for their breakfast provide a delightful start to your weekend. After breakfast, make your way down to the beach. The sea is warm and calm in the morning, and swimming before it becomes busy is a pleasure in itself. Spend an hour in the water and another soaking up the sun. This simple routine is all it takes to leave the week's fatigue behind. Saturday Afternoon: A Walk Through History You can dedicate Saturday to discovering Cyprus' historical and natural treasures. Setting off early after breakfast gives you enough time to visit several major attractions located within easy reach of one another. Kyrenia Castle and the Old Harbour Your first stop of the day could be Kyrenia Castle. Dating back to the Byzantine period, it is one of the island's most significant historical landmarks and is home to the Shipwreck Museum. Climb to the castle walls to enjoy panoramic views over Kyrenia Harbour. Bellapais Abbey Just a few minutes from Kyrenia, Bellapais Village is a must-visit destination, known for its stone houses and peaceful atmosphere. Renowned for its striking Gothic architecture, Bellapais Abbey is a favourite with both history enthusiasts and photographers. Before continuing your journey, stop for a coffee at one of the cafés in the village square. The Walled City of Nicosia

Around midday, continue to the historic heart of Nicosia. Büyük Han, Kumarcılar Han, Selimiye Mosque and Arasta Bazaar are all within easy walking distance of one another. As you wander through the narrow streets, you'll come across buildings that reflect both the Ottoman and Venetian periods, as well as shops selling traditional local handicrafts. Famagusta and the Ancient City of Salamis

If time allows, continue your journey to Famagusta in the afternoon. The Ancient City of Salamis, with its theatre, colonnaded streets and Roman baths, is one of Cyprus' most impressive archaeological sites. Afterwards, take a walk through the historic walled city and visit Lala Mustafa Pasha Mosque and the remains of the Venetian Palace. An Evening Recommendation After a full day of sightseeing, enjoy dinner by the sea or return to your hotel to relax and make the most of its spa, pool and entertainment facilities. During the summer months, many Northern Cyprus hotels also host live music, stage performances and open-air events. You May Also Like: The Best Places to Visit in Cyprus Sunday Morning: A Relaxed Finish Before your journey home on Sunday, slow the pace and take the opportunity to experience Cyprus' local way of life. A short outing and a little shopping after breakfast are the perfect way to round off your weekend break. Shopping for Local Products Around Arasta Bazaar in Nicosia, Kyrenia Bazaar, and Büyük Han, you'll find a wide selection of authentic Cypriot products. Handmade ceramics, Lefkara embroidery, olive oil products, natural soaps, halloumi cheese, Cypriot jams, and Turkish delight are among the island's most popular souvenirs. Modern Shopping Centres If you prefer modern shopping, the shopping centres in Nicosia are home to both international brands and local boutiques. Before heading home, you can pick up anything you need or buy a few gifts for family and friends. A Coffee Break by the Sea If your flight schedule allows, enjoy a coffee overlooking the sea at Kyrenia Harbour or along the Alsancak coastline. The harbour is particularly peaceful in the morning, making it an ideal place for a relaxing walk while enjoying the final hours of your weekend against the backdrop of the Mediterranean. One Last Stop Before You Leave Before heading to the airport, stop by a local delicatessen or market to pick up regional favourites such as Cypriot coffee, olives, citrus preserves, or locally produced wines. That way, you'll leave your short weekend break with not only wonderful memories but also thoughtful gifts to take home. Although it's only a short itinerary, careful planning allows you to explore historic towns, relax along the Mediterranean coast, sample local flavours, and enjoy a little shopping—all within a memorable Northern Cyprus weekend holiday. Practical Tips for a Holiday in Cyprus A little preparation will make your weekend getaway much smoother. As your time is limited, planning efficiently is essential. Pack a cabin bag instead of checked luggage to avoid wasting time waiting at baggage reclaim. Arrange your flights and airport transfers in advance. If you plan to explore the island, hire a car and remember that traffic drives on the left. Bring sunscreen, a hat, and comfortable walking shoes. Turkish Lira is accepted in most places, but it's still a good idea to keep your bank card handy. Plan a full itinerary for Saturday and keep Sunday more relaxed to make your journey home easier. These small preparations can make a two-day trip feel much longer. Even a short Cyprus holiday can be incredibly rewarding when planned well. Frequently Asked Questions About a Holiday in Cyprus How many days are enough for a weekend break in Cyprus? Two full days are an ideal starting point. If you arrive on Friday evening and return on Sunday evening, you'll have enough time to experience the island without rushing. If you can add an extra day, you'll also have time to visit more distant destinations such as the Karpaz Peninsula. What is the best season for a weekend in Cyprus? Spring and autumn are the best times to visit. The sea is still warm, the weather is ideal for walking and sightseeing, and prices are generally lower than during the peak summer season. April to May and September to October are the most balanced periods for a short getaway. Which area is the best place to stay in Cyprus? Kyrenia is the best choice for a lively weekend, while Famagusta is ideal for a quieter escape. Among Cyprus hotels, beachfront properties allow you to make the most of a short stay and enjoy everything the island has to offer.

Do I need a visa for a holiday in Northern Cyprus? Visitors travelling from Türkiye can enter Northern Cyprus with either a passport or a national ID card.
