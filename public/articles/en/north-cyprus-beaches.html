If you’d like, let’s explore Cyprus’s most beautiful beaches together! Nissi Beach Located in Ayia Napa, Nissi Beach is widely regarded as one of the best beaches in Cyprus. This stunning coastline, stretching approximately 500 meters, captivates visitors from the very first step with its soft white sand and vibrant atmosphere. Its crystal-clear turquoise waters have earned the prestigious Blue Flag award, marking it as a premium Cyprus beach for cleanliness and quality.

Nissi Beach gained global recognition in 2002, when live broadcasts and DJ performances by the BBC Radio 1 Roadshow brought international fame to its shores.

Today, it remains a hotspot for energy and entertainment. Throughout the summer, the beach comes alive with foam parties and world-class live music events. For those seeking action, this iconic Cyprus beach also offers adrenaline-filled water sports and public beach volleyball courts.

Golden Beach

Golden Beach is a pristine public beach in Northern Cyprus that truly lives up to its name with its vast stretch of shimmering golden sand. Located near Rizokarpaso on the Karpaz Peninsula, this beach is a vital nesting ground for Caretta Caretta turtles. As a protected area, development remains strictly limited, giving it a special status among Cyprus beaches and preserving its untouched natural beauty.

This serene setting makes Golden Beach the ultimate destination for those seeking tranquility. You can take long, meditative walks along the seemingly endless shoreline and enjoy a peaceful swim in its transparent waters. Its romantic atmosphere makes it particularly popular among honeymooners, couples, and nature enthusiasts.

While Golden Beach is accessible by road from various parts of the island, the most scenic route is through the Karpaz Peninsula by private or rental car. The journey itself, filled with Mediterranean vistas, is as much a part of the experience as the destination.

With its distinctive charm, Golden Beach holds a prominent position among the best beaches in Cyprus.

Karpaz Beach

Stretching along the pristine Karpaz Peninsula, Karpaz Beach is a vast coastal treasure comprising multiple interconnected shores and bays. As one of the most untouched Cyprus beaches, this area offers a diverse range of coastal experiences. The primary spots that define Karpaz Beach include

Golden Beach: Famous for its vast dunes of shimmering sand and crystal-clear waters, it is also a protected nesting ground for Caretta Caretta turtles. Ronnas Beach: A secluded gem known for its absolute tranquility, offering a peaceful retreat far from the bustling crowds. Ayios Philon Beach: An unspoiled stretch of sand popular among nature lovers and those seeking a more natural experience. It stands next to the historical Ayios Philon Church.
