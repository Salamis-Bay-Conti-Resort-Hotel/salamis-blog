Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do Early Booking Deals Include a Price Guarantee?

Not necessarily. Price guarantees depend on the terms of the offer and the booking channel. Before reserving, check the conditions relating to price changes and whether the quoted rate is subject to any restrictions.

2. Can You Change Your Room Type After Booking?

Changing your room type depends on availability and the terms of your reservation. Contact the hotel or booking provider to find out whether a change is possible and whether any additional payment or amendment fee applies.

3. Are Early Booking Deals Available for Families Travelling with Children?

Some offers may be suitable for families, but eligibility conditions vary. Age limits, the number of children permitted and room occupancy rules may differ between packages. When booking, provide accurate details for every guest and confirm that the selected room can accommodate your party.

4. How Can You Check the Latest Hotel Offers and Their Terms?

Check the hotel's official announcements, contact its reservations team directly or consult the authorised booking channel advertising the offer. Before making a reservation, it is advisable to confirm the price, eligible dates and included services in writing.

5. What Should You Check Before Paying for an Early Booking?

Review the total price, payment schedule, additional charges, cancellation conditions and refund policy. Once payment is complete, keep your booking confirmation and check that the travel dates, room type, guest details and agreed terms are correct.
