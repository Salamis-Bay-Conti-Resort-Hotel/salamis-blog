Boat Tour Routes in Cyprus

With its turquoise bays, unspoilt beaches, and spectacular coastline, Cyprus is one of the Mediterranean's most enjoyable destinations for boat tours. From Famagusta to Kyrenia, and from the Karpaz Peninsula to secluded coves, each route offers an unforgettable day at sea, complete with swimming stops, snorkelling opportunities, and breathtaking scenery. Whether you're planning a full-day boat trip or a sunset cruise, every route reveals a different side of the island's natural beauty. In this guide, you'll discover the most popular boat tour routes in Cyprus and the key factors to consider when choosing the right one. Kyrenia Coast The moment your boat leaves the harbour, the stone silhouette of Kyrenia Castle begins to fade into the distance behind you. As you cruise further along the coast, olive groves, rocky coves, and the dramatic mountain range meeting the sea unfold before your eyes. The Kyrenia Coast is one of the most peaceful boat tour routes in Northern Cyprus. The water is crystal clear, the bays are calm, and there are plenty of places where you can drop anchor and dive into the refreshing sea. Stretching out on deck beneath the midday sun before slipping into the turquoise water creates a rhythm that quietly shapes the rest of your day. This route is an excellent choice for first-time boat tour guests. Its short distances, sheltered waters, and close proximity to the shore make for a relaxed introduction to life at sea. If you're planning a Northern Cyprus holiday, a boat tour departing from Kyrenia deserves a place at the top of your itinerary. Karpaz Peninsula

As you travel towards the island's north-eastern tip, the crowds gradually disappear behind you. The Karpaz Peninsula is one of the few places where nature still takes centre stage, with golden sandy shores and exceptionally clear waters. Sailing to Karpaz offers the chance to experience Cyprus at its most peaceful. Secluded bays, beaches where wild donkeys roam freely, and tranquil waters framed by swaying reeds create an unforgettable setting. Dropping anchor off Golden Beach before swimming in the warm Mediterranean becomes one of the most memorable moments of the day. This route takes a little longer, but the experience is well worth the journey. Combining simplicity with remarkable natural beauty, Karpaz is perfect for anyone seeking complete relaxation at sea. Most boat tours around the peninsula last a full day, so remember to bring a hat, sunscreen, and plenty of patience. Famagusta Bay Imagine setting sail beneath the shadow of ancient city walls. Famagusta Bay is one of the few boat tour routes where history and the sea come together so effortlessly. As your boat moves away from the shore, you'll be greeted by the outline of the Ancient City of Salamis reflected against the sea, long sandy beaches, and shallow waters that warm quickly under the Mediterranean sun. The water is so clear that you can easily see the sandy seabed beneath the boat. With gentle waves and sheltered bays, it's also an excellent choice for families travelling with children. Famagusta Bay is a place where history and the sea share the same horizon. If you're looking to swim, sunbathe, and admire Cyprus' rich heritage from the water, this route is an ideal choice. For anyone hoping to combine history and the sea during their Cyprus holiday, Famagusta Bay is one of the island's finest boat tour destinations. Ayia Napa As you make your way to the southern coast, the scenery begins to change. Off the shores of Ayia Napa, you'll find sea caves and natural rock arches carved into the coastline. As the boat glides between these remarkable formations, sunlight reflects on the water, filling the caves with a stunning turquoise glow. Known as the Sea Caves, this area is a peaceful haven for photography enthusiasts. Each cave takes on a different shade depending on the angle of the sun. Boat captains often stop here, giving you the chance to dive into the water and cool off in the shade of the caves. The route usually continues as far as Cape Greco. Crystal-clear waters, dramatic headlands, and sheltered bays perfect for snorkelling make this one of the island's most rewarding boat tours. It's an excellent choice for anyone wanting to experience the coastline shaped by the sea at close range. Akamas Coast

On the western side of the island, the Akamas Peninsula offers a completely different experience with its unspoilt natural beauty. As your boat approaches the coastline, the water gradually becomes lighter and clearer until it resembles glass in front of the famous Blue Lagoon. Once you drop anchor and step into the water, you'll be able to see the sandy seabed beneath you and schools of fish swimming all around. The surroundings are peaceful, the coastline remains untouched, and the warm, inviting sea is perfect for a swim. Even a short snorkelling session here feels like a small adventure. Most Akamas boat tours depart from Latsi Harbour. It's an ideal route for those who want to spend a simple, relaxing day surrounded by nature. If you're looking to escape the crowds and enjoy a peaceful day at sea, this is the place to be. Famagusta Blue Lagoon Located off the coast of Famagusta, Famagusta Blue Lagoon is one of the most popular stops on boat tours thanks to its crystal-clear turquoise waters and peaceful atmosphere. Its location close to the shore makes it a favourite on day cruises, offering ideal conditions for swimming and snorkelling. The lagoon's exceptional water clarity, particularly during the summer months, allows you to see much of the seabed with ease. As you relax in the sun and enjoy the sea throughout the day, you'll experience the Mediterranean from an entirely different perspective. Cape Kormakitis Situated in the north-west of Northern Cyprus, Cape Kormakitis is known for its unspoilt coastline and peaceful coves. It's an ideal destination for anyone looking to enjoy a quieter boat tour away from the crowds, while its rugged shoreline and clear waters also provide spectacular views for photographers. You can stop for a swim in one of the small coves accessible only by boat and fully appreciate the tranquil atmosphere of the Mediterranean. At sunset, the area around the cape becomes particularly breathtaking, creating unforgettable memories. Aphrodite Beach

Steeped in mythology, Aphrodite Beach is one of Cyprus' most iconic stretches of coastline. According to legend, this is where Aphrodite, the goddess of love and beauty, emerged from the sea. Today, the beach continues to captivate visitors with its natural beauty. A favourite stop on many boat tours, it is known for its crystal-clear waters and striking rock formations. Here, you can enjoy a swimming break, explore the underwater world while snorkelling, and spend time at one of the Mediterranean's most beautiful coastal locations. Lazaretta Island Located just off the coast of Famagusta, Lazaretta Island adds a unique dimension to any boat tour with its fascinating history and calm waters. Small and uninhabited, the island is ideal for swimming and snorkelling thanks to its exceptional water clarity. The surrounding marine life and natural rock formations offer plenty to discover beneath the surface. Easily included in day trips departing from Famagusta, Lazaretta Island is one of Cyprus' most distinctive stops for anyone wishing to enjoy a peaceful day surrounded by nature. You May Also Like: The Most Beautiful Beaches in Northern Cyprus Choosing the Right Boat Tour in Cyprus A few simple considerations before setting sail can make your day even more enjoyable. Here are the key points to keep in mind: Duration: Half-day tours are shorter and more relaxed, while longer routes such as the Karpaz Peninsula require a full day. Type of boat: Small yachts offer a quieter and more private experience, while larger catamarans provide a livelier, more social atmosphere. Number of guests: Choose smaller group tours if you're looking for a peaceful day at sea, or opt for larger groups if you prefer a more vibrant experience. What's included: Check whether lunch, drinks, and snorkelling equipment are included in the tour price. Departure point: Choosing a tour that departs from a harbour close to where you're staying allows you to make the most of your day. One final tip: morning tours usually enjoy calmer seas. Winds tend to pick up later in the day, making earlier departures a better choice for both comfort and scenery. Tips for a Day at Sea A little preparation will make your boat trip even more enjoyable. Before spending hours out on the water, keep these practical tips in mind: Keep your sunscreen, hat, and sunglasses at the top of your bag for easy access. Wear shoes with non-slip soles to move safely around the deck. Pack a small towel and a spare swimsuit to stay comfortable after swimming. Use a waterproof phone case to protect your device from salt water. If you're prone to seasickness, have a light meal before departure and bring medication if necessary. Once you're prepared, everything else falls into place naturally. Let yourself drift with the breeze and allow the rhythm of the sea to shape your day.
