What to Do in Cyprus in One Week?

Planning a getaway to a beautiful island is always a huge thrill, but sometimes the hardest part is just figuring out where to start. If you are getting ready for your Cyprus travel adventure, you likely want to see as much as you can without feeling like you are constantly in a hurry. One week is a perfect amount of time to get a real feel for the culture, see the historic sites, and still have plenty of hours to lounge by the sea. Let us look at an itinerary that helps you see the best of the island while keeping the energy high and the mood relaxed. Knowing the best things to do in Cyprus ahead of time will help you make every moment count.

Day 1: Explore Famagusta!

Kick off your first day by heading straight to Famagusta to explore one of the most fascinating corners of the island. The whole place feels like a living history book that you can actually walk through. You really can't miss the ancient city of Salamis. These ruins sit right by the sea, where you can wander through old Roman theaters and check out the tall columns stretching up toward the bright blue sky. Once you’ve had your fill of the ruins, make your way over to the old walled city of Famagusta.

The massive Venetian walls are still standing today, and when you step inside, the narrow streets are full of life and character. When you are wondering what to do in Cyprus on your first day, this mix of ancient ruins and living history is the perfect choice. You will also want to catch a glimpse of the Varosha area, which has its own moving story.

Day 2: The Coastal Towns of Kyrenia

For your second day, take a trip to the north coast to see Kyrenia. Many visitors agree that this is the best place to go in Cyprus if you love a cozy atmosphere with a breathtaking view. While you are in Kyrenia, make sure you go to Kyrenia Castle. It sits right at the entrance of the harbor and has a very cool shipwreck museum inside. After you explore the castle, you can head up into the mountains to visit Bellapais Abbey. Walking through the gardens of the abbey is a great way to relax and take some beautiful photos.

Day 3: Tour the Karpaz Peninsula

If you want to see the wild and natural side of the island, you need to head to the Karpaz Peninsula. This is a long, narrow finger of land that feels like a different world. This is one of the top things to do in Cyprus with family because of the famous wild donkeys that live there.

At the very end of the peninsula, you will find Golden Beach. It is a huge stretch of sand with turquoise water that looks like something from a postcard. If you are on the hunt for things to do in Cyprus with kids, a day out at this wild beach is a total win. You can splash in the waves, build big sandcastles, and just soak up the sun in a place that feels completely natural and tucked away.

Day 4: Discover Nicosia

For day four, make your way to Nicosia, the only divided capital left in the world. It is a high-energy, bustling city right in the center of the island. One of the best things to do in Cyprus is wandering through the narrow streets of the old town and walking across the Green Line at Ledra Street. You will find plenty of tiny shops overflowing with local spices and handmade crafts to explore. Plus, make sure you visit the Büyük Han, which is a beautiful old inn from the Ottoman period.

Day 5: A Relaxing Day in Larnaca

After all that exploring, it is time for a slower pace in Larnaca. This city has a very famous palm-lined promenade called Finikoudes, where you can walk and enjoy the sea breeze. One of the best things to do in Cyprus on a quiet day is to visit the Salt Lake. If you are there at the right time of year, you might even see the flamingos that stop by for a visit.

You can also visit the Church of Saint Lazarus, which is a very important and beautiful stone building in the center of the town. The streets around the church are full of traditional taverns and little shops. Larnaca is often considered the best place to go in Cyprus when you want a mix of city life and a relaxed beach vibe.

Day 6: A Tour of Cyprus' Southern Region

Day six is for heading further south toward Limassol and Paphos. There are so many things to do in Paphos, Cyprus, that you could spend days there, but you should definitely start with the famous mosaics. They are located in an archaeological park right by the harbor and show incredible scenes from ancient mythology.

You also have to stop at Aphrodite’s Rock. According to the old stories, this is where the Goddess of Love was born from the sea. It is a very popular spot for swimming and taking photos against the big white rocks and the blue waves. If you are looking for what to do in Cyprus to see the natural beauty of the south, this is it. The variety of things to do in Paphos, Cyprus, makes it a hit with everyone in the family, from the history lovers to those who just want to be by the sea.

Day 7: Rest and Shopping on Your Last Day

On your last day, you should keep things very simple. You have seen a lot of the island, so now is the time to go back to your favorite beach or find a local market. Finding things to do in Cyprus with family on your final day should be about relaxing together. You can look for some local honey, halloumi cheese, or traditional sweets to take home as a memory of your trip. It is a great time to just enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle.
