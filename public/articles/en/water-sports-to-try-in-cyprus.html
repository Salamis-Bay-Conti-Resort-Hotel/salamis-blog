A Thrilling Day on the Mediterranean with Jet Skiing

Jet skiing is one of the most energetic activities that comes to mind when thinking about water sports in Cyprus. Jet ski services may be available at various coastal locations for those looking to travel across the water at speed. One of the most enjoyable aspects of the activity is being able to see the coastline from a completely different perspective. A short jet-ski session after a classic day at the beach can add a different pace to your holiday. However, when taking part in motorised water sports such as jet skiing, it is important to follow the operating conditions and safety rules set by the provider. Details such as age restrictions, designated operating areas and weather conditions may vary between providers, so it is advisable to check the requirements before taking part.

See Cyprus from Above with Parasailing

Parasailing is one of the activities that allows you to rise above the sea and enjoy a bird's-eye view of the coastline. Cyprus's turquoise waters and long coastline can look completely different from above. As you rise above sea level, beaches, boats and the coastline can be viewed from a much wider perspective. Parasailing can also create a memorable holiday experience for those who enjoy photography. Before taking part, however, it is important to check the provider's safety procedures and the current weather conditions.

Diving in Cyprus

Cyprus's location in the Mediterranean also makes the island an appealing destination for diving enthusiasts. Observing marine life, rocky areas and underwater formations beneath the surface offers a different experience for those looking to go beyond a traditional beach holiday. Beginners may be able to take part in introductory training or guided diving experiences, while experienced divers can research suitable dive sites in advance according to their travel itinerary. During a dive, it is important not to touch marine life, damage natural formations or disturb underwater ecosystems. This allows visitors to explore the natural beauty of Cyprus while helping to preserve its marine environment.

A Peaceful Experience on the Water with Paddleboarding

Not every water sport involves speed and adrenaline. Stand Up Paddleboarding, commonly known as SUP, is an option for those who want to spend time on the water at a more relaxed pace. Standing on a paddleboard and moving across the water allows you to enjoy the coastline from a different perspective. The experience can be particularly enjoyable when the sea is calm. Paddleboarding in the early morning can be a pleasant option for those who want to experience the Mediterranean atmosphere of Cyprus in a more peaceful setting.

Explore the Coastline by Kayak

Kayaking is another water sport to consider for those who want to stay active while moving across the water at a more controlled pace. Paddling along the coastline provides an opportunity to discover beaches and sea views from a different perspective. Sea and weather conditions should be taken into account when kayaking. Those trying it for the first time may benefit from professional guidance, helping them enjoy the activity in a more controlled manner. After kayaking, taking a walk along the beach or relaxing by the sea can be a good way to balance the pace of the day.

Water Skiing and Wakeboarding

Water skiing and wakeboarding are other options for those looking for more energetic water sports. Both activities involve being towed behind a boat and require balance and coordination. If you are trying either activity for the first time, it can be useful to check in advance whether beginner-level instruction or professional guidance is available. Sea conditions are also important for these activities, so it is advisable to follow the provider's latest updates for the day you plan to participate.

Discover the Coastline of Cyprus by Boat

Although boat trips are not technically a water sport, they are one of the enjoyable ways to add variety to your experience by the sea in Cyprus. During a day boat trip, you may be able to enjoy coastal views from the water, take swimming breaks or watch the sunset from the boat. Sunset boat trips can be a romantic evening option for couples, while families may prefer day trips with swimming stops and a more relaxed route. When planning a boat trip, it is worth checking the duration, route and whether the itinerary may change depending on weather conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions About Water Sports to Try in Cyprus

How far in advance should you book water sports in Cyprus?

Especially during the summer season and busy holiday periods, booking popular water sports in advance can be a good idea. As booking requirements vary depending on the activity, location and provider, it is advisable to check the details before your travel date. Guests staying in Famagusta can also ask Salamis Bay Conti Resort Hotel or relevant local providers about the latest activity options available in the area.

Can you hire water sports equipment in Cyprus?

Many water sports providers may supply the basic equipment required for an activity. However, the equipment included can vary depending on the activity and provider. Before booking, it is therefore worth checking which equipment is included in the price and which items you are expected to bring yourself.

Are there age restrictions for water sports in Cyprus?

Age restrictions vary depending on the activity and the provider's rules. Motorised water sports, parasailing and certain towing activities may have specific age, height or weight requirements. Families planning activities with children should check the provider's current requirements before booking.

Where can you take diving lessons in Cyprus?

Diving centres in Cyprus may offer a range of courses, from beginner to advanced levels. When choosing a course, it is important to consider the qualifications of the instructor or centre, the content of the programme and the equipment provided. Those diving for the first time should consider programmes designed for beginners.

What should you wear for water sports in Cyprus?

Depending on the activity, swimwear or swim shorts, quick-drying clothing, sun protection and water-friendly footwear can be useful. Special equipment or clothing required for diving and certain water sports may be provided by the activity operator. For activities involving prolonged exposure to the sun, it can also be helpful to bring a hat and sun protection.

Can water sports in Cyprus be cancelled due to weather conditions?

Yes. Wind, wave height, rainfall and other weather conditions can affect whether certain water sports can be carried out safely. Activity providers may therefore change the scheduled time or cancel an activity after assessing the weather conditions. Keeping your travel itinerary flexible can make it easier to arrange an alternative activity if plans change.
