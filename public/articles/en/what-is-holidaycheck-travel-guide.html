What Is HolidayCheck? A Guide for Travelers Planning Their Next Vacation

A little doubt creeps in before you book a hotel: Do the photos tell the truth? Is the sea really that close? Is breakfast worth waking up for? HolidayCheck exists for exactly those people looking for answers. HolidayCheck is a hotel review and holiday booking platform. As the largest hotel review portal in German-speaking countries, it gathers real travelers' experiences under one roof. Millions of reviews and photos let you base your next trip on facts rather than guesswork. In this guide, you'll learn how the platform works, how its rating system runs, what the awards mean, and how to read reviews the smart way. We'll also show you how to use this tool when planning a holiday in Cyprus. Our goal is simple: to help you book with confidence. How Does HolidayCheck Work?

The heart of HolidayCheck is the reviews left by real guests. The platform started as a small hobby project back in 2000, and today it hosts more than 13 million hotel reviews. Add over 16 million holiday photos, and you can see hotels from every angle. The rating system works on a scale of 6. When a hotel scores something like 5.8/6, that signals an almost flawless experience. Guests rate hotels separately across categories such as cleanliness, service, location, rooms, and food. There's also a recommendation percentage. A figure like 97%, for example, means the vast majority of reviewers would suggest the hotel to others. Bottom line: Looking at all three indicators together, rather than a single score, gives you the most accurate picture. Hotel Search and Booking Features HolidayCheck isn't just a review site. It's also a powerful booking and price comparison tool. Its portfolio holds more than 4 million hotels worldwide. The platform compares prices from over 100 tour operators and finds the best offer for you. It brings package holidays with flights, last-minute deals, hotel-only bookings, and car rentals together on a single screen. While searching, you can use filters for dates, number of guests, concept, and budget. That way, you quickly narrow thousands of options down to the ones that fit you. When you focus on a specific area like Cyprus hotels, the filtering feature wraps up your search in minutes. The HolidayCheck Award System When you spot the "HolidayCheck Award" badge on a hotel, it's no coincidence. This award goes to hotels that have earned high ratings from guests over a long stretch of time. The most prestigious tier is the HolidayCheck Gold Award. To win it, a hotel must receive excellent holiday reviews for at least 5 consecutive years. So it's not a short-lived success, but a sign of consistent quality. These awards offer you a practical shortcut. If you don't have time to read hundreds of reviews one by one, award-winning hotels make a safe starting point. In short: The award is concrete proof of years of guest satisfaction. How to Read Reviews Effectively Reviews are worth their weight in gold, but only when read correctly. Getting stuck on a single negative comment, or looking only at the overall score, can mislead you. Here's a smarter way to read them: Focus on the majority. Instead of one or two extreme reviews, look for recurring themes. If the same praise or complaint shows up across many reviews, it's probably real. Check the date. Recent reviews come first. Hotels change over time, and a criticism from two years ago may no longer apply. Find your own priority. If you're a family, filter the reviews mentioning kids; if you're a couple, look for notes on peace and views. Trust the photos. Guest photos give a more honest picture than a hotel's professional shots. These few habits turn a pile of reviews into a meaningful guide. That way, you minimize the gap between your expectations and reality. Comparing Hotels on the Platform

When more than one hotel catches your eye, comparing them helps you make the right call. HolidayCheck makes this process intuitive. Place two hotels side by side and you'll see their scores, recommendation rates, concept, and price all at once. So you answer the "which one is better" question with data, not emotion. A small tip: Don't just chase the highest score—weigh the value for money too. Sometimes a hotel rated 5.4 can be a better pick than one rated 5.8, thanks to its concept and location. So comparison gives you the chance to optimize both your money and your holiday. Tips for Catching the Best Deals Finding the right hotel is half the job; getting it at a good price is the other half. A few small moves noticeably lighten your budget. Here's a checklist that works: Book early. Popular Cyprus hotels fill up fast in the summer months; those who move early both find a spot and save money. Follow last-minute deals. If your calendar is flexible, discounted packages ease the pressure on your wallet. Use price comparison. The same hotel can be priced differently across various operators. The platform finds the cheapest one for you. Consider the off-season. In spring and autumn, the sea is still warm while prices sit below summer levels. Don't miss cashback and campaigns. Some properties have extra refund offers waiting for you. Even if you apply just a few of these steps, you can experience the same holiday on a smaller budget. In summary: A patient, comparison-driven search is the key to the best price.
