The Most Instagrammable Places in Cyprus

With its Mediterranean coastline, historic landmarks, stone-paved streets and natural beauty, Cyprus offers a variety of settings for travel photography. From the historic scenery of Kyrenia Harbour and the architectural details of Bellapais Abbey to the panoramic views from St Hilarion Castle and the natural beaches of the Karpaz Peninsula, there are plenty of places across the island where you can create Instagram-worthy content. Salamis Bay Conti Resort Hotel, located on the beachfront in the Famagusta region of Cyprus, can also be considered by those looking to combine a seaside holiday with the opportunity to explore the island's historic and natural attractions.

Kyrenia Harbour

Kyrenia Harbour is one of the most popular locations for Instagram photography in Cyprus. Historic buildings, boats moored in the harbour, stone architecture and Mediterranean views come together to create a variety of photo compositions in the same area. During the day, the sea and boats take centre stage, while the changing light later in the day can give the harbour a completely different atmosphere. Rather than limiting your photography route to the harbour itself, exploring the surrounding streets can add more variety to your travel album.

Bellapais Abbey

Located near Kyrenia, Bellapais Abbey is one of the key landmarks to include in an itinerary for anyone interested in photographing the historic character of Cyprus. The building reflects elements of Gothic architecture and offers a range of opportunities for architectural photography with its stone walls, arches and courtyard. Alongside wide-angle shots, you can also create more detailed close-up photographs focusing on the arches, stone textures and architectural features. Visiting Bellapais is not only an opportunity to take photographs but also a way to experience Cyprus's historic heritage at close range.

St Hilarion Castle

Located in the Kyrenia mountain range, St Hilarion Castle is a memorable addition to a photography itinerary thanks to its historic atmosphere and panoramic views from its elevated position. The castle walls, stone stairways, arched passages and surrounding mountain scenery provide a variety of angles and compositions. Due to its elevated location, visiting during the cooler parts of the day can make the experience more comfortable, particularly in warm weather. Comfortable footwear is also recommended for exploring the site.

Ancient City of Salamis

Located near Famagusta, the Ancient City of Salamis is an important destination for those looking to add history and culture to their Cyprus holiday. Ancient columns, the theatre and stone ruins create a visual atmosphere that differs from typical beach and seaside photographs. Wide-angle shots can help capture the scale of the ancient structures together with their natural surroundings. For visitors staying in Famagusta, the Ancient City of Salamis also provides an opportunity to combine a seaside holiday with a cultural and historical excursion.

Karpaz Peninsula and Golden Beach

The Karpaz Peninsula offers a different experience for those looking to photograph the natural beauty of Cyprus. Its long beaches, unspoilt scenery and Mediterranean views provide plenty of opportunities for nature- and beach-themed Instagram content. Rather than focusing solely on photographs of the sea, you can also capture roads and landscapes, details along the shore, local vegetation and sunsets to create more varied content. Golden Beach, in particular, can be ideal for simpler compositions thanks to its light-coloured sand and wide horizon. Calm beach scenes can be photographed in the morning, while the changing light around sunset creates a different atmosphere and range of tones.

Famagusta's City Walls and Historic City

Famagusta offers a variety of options for creating a photography route thanks to its historic landmarks and distinctive urban character. The city walls, stone buildings, gateways and narrow streets create numerous photo opportunities that can be explored on foot. Lala Mustafa Pasha Mosque is another notable landmark within Famagusta's historic city centre. Its façade, stonework and architectural details can be photographed from a range of angles. When taking photographs at historic or religious sites, it is important to follow visitor guidelines and respect the privacy of those using places of worship. This helps ensure a considerate photography experience that respects the cultural significance of the location.

Beach and Sunset Photography in Cyprus

You do not necessarily need to visit a specific tourist attraction to create Instagram content. Across Cyprus, the sea, sky and horizon can be used to create simple and atmospheric photographs at different beaches. The softer light in the morning and late afternoon can help create a different look, particularly in seaside photography. Arriving before sunset also gives you the opportunity to capture changes in the colours of the sky at different stages. For couples travelling together, photographing romantic moments can make an Instagram feed more personal, while families can capture everyday activities and shared experiences from their holiday.

Ideas for Natural Instagram Photos in Cyprus

Not every photograph taken during a Cyprus holiday needs to look like a professional photoshoot. Everyday moments, such as walking through a historic street, enjoying a coffee by the sea, reading a book on the beach or watching the sunset, can add a more natural feel to your travel story. Small details can also become part of your photo collection, from a restaurant table setting and a historic doorway to boats in the harbour or decorative features on a building. This allows you to create more varied content that captures the overall atmosphere of your trip rather than focusing only on major tourist attractions.

How to Create a Photography Route in Cyprus

Instead of trying to visit all of Cyprus's photography locations in a single day, exploring different areas on separate days can make for a more relaxed travel itinerary. For example, one day in Famagusta could be dedicated to the Ancient City of Salamis and the historic city centre, while another could focus on coastal locations. Around Kyrenia, Kyrenia Harbour, Bellapais Abbey and St Hilarion Castle can be considered as part of the same sightseeing itinerary. Respecting Nature and Heritage While Taking Photographs

When creating content for Instagram, it is important to help preserve the places you visit. Avoiding climbing on historic structures, refraining from behaviour that could damage archaeological remains and taking care of natural areas are all important aspects of responsible travel photography. Not leaving rubbish on beaches, avoiding disturbance to wildlife and following the rules in protected areas are also important considerations when travelling and taking photographs.

Frequently Asked Questions About the Most Instagrammable Places in Cyprus

Which season is best for Instagram photos in Cyprus?

The best season depends on the type of content you want to create. The warmer months can be suitable for beach and seaside photography, while spring and autumn may appeal to those looking for a calmer atmosphere and softer natural light.

What time of day is best for photography in Cyprus?

Early morning and the period before sunset can be ideal for photography because natural light tends to be softer. You can also experiment with different times of day to capture the same location in different atmospheres.

What should you consider when taking photographs at historic sites in Cyprus?

Visitors should follow the photography rules of each location, avoid touching historic structures and respect other visitors. At religious sites, it is also important to take account of areas used for worship and follow any specific visitor guidelines.

Which natural areas can be included in a photography itinerary in Cyprus?

The Karpaz Peninsula, Golden Beach and various coastal locations can be included in a natural photography itinerary. Alongside sea views, sunsets, road scenes and details along the coastline can add variety to a travel album.

What equipment can be used for travel photography in Cyprus?

Smartphones are a practical option for travel photography, while compact or professional cameras can provide additional shooting possibilities. If you are planning to spend the whole day exploring, lightweight equipment can be more convenient. It is also useful to have sufficient storage space and a portable charger or spare battery.

Can you take photographs with a drone in Cyprus?

Drone use may be subject to different rules depending on the location and the nature of the area where you plan to fly. Restrictions may apply near airports, military sites, protected areas and crowded locations. Before flying, it is important to check the applicable local regulations and whether any permits are required.
