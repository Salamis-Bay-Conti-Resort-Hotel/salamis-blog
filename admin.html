How to format your content
  • First line = main title (used for <h1> and the page title if no override).
  • Kapak Görsel: https://... (or just a URL on the line right after the title) = cover image inside <header>.
  • Lines that end with : become section titles (<h2>).
  • Any standalone URL line becomes a <figure> with auto alt + caption.
  • Blank lines separate paragraphs.
